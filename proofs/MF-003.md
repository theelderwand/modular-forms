# Identity MF-003: Three-Term Eta-Quotient Relation

## Statement

```
  η(1z)⁻² η(2z)² η(3z)² η(4z)⁻¹ η(6z)⁴ η(8z) η(12z)⁻³ η(24z)
  -2·η(1z)⁻¹ η(3z)³ η(4z)⁻² η(8z)³ η(12z)² η(24z)⁻¹
  + η(2z)⁻¹ η(3z)⁴ η(6z) η(8z) η(12z)⁻² η(24z)
  = 0
```

**Weight:** 2 | **Level:** 24 | **Character:** trivial

## Proof

### Step 1: Modularity
Each term is an eta-quotient with `d | 24`, weight `(1/2)∑e_d = 2`,
satisfying Newman's criteria and Ligozat holomorphicity — hence in
`M_2(Γ₀(24))` with trivial character.

### Step 2: Sturm Bound
[SL₂(Z):Γ₀(24)] = 48, so B = (2/12)·48 = 8.

### Step 3: Verification
The q-expansion vanishes through `q^8` (verified to `q⁵⁰⁰`).
By Sturm, the combination is identically zero. ∎
